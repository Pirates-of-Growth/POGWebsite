Questions we get

Straight answers.

How many hours is that?

We scope by deliverable and outcome. You get a list of what lands each month and what it does for you. Hours don't tell you whether it worked.

What if it doesn't work?

The blueprint is free and defines success up front. Each month stands on its own, you evaluate it, and you decide whether to continue.

We're stretched on our side. Can we still do this?

Yes, if we plan for it. We state what we need from you up front, such as definitions, feedback rounds and handling replies within a day, and plan the work around it.

Do you only work with HubSpot?

We are HubSpot-first. The ICP, signals, enrichment and architecture work can run on other CRMs, but we only sell full builds on HubSpot.

Won't more outbound burn our accounts?

Signal-based lists are smaller and sharper, so you contact fewer people with a better reason. We plan volumes, sending domains and data handling with deliverability and GDPR in mind from the start.

Where do you work?

From Amsterdam, with clients across the Benelux, DACH, the Nordics, and the UK and Ireland.
